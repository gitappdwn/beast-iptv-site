How to Set Up IPTV on Samsung Tizen & LG webOS Smart TVs

Published: September 12, 2026 Author: Beast iptv 5 Min Read

Streaming live TV, movies, and on‑demand series directly on your smart TV is no longer a luxury—it’s a standard expectation. With Beast IPTV you can turn any Samsung Tizen or LG webOS TV into a full‑featured entertainment hub without a set‑top box. This guide walks you through the entire process, from checking hardware compatibility to troubleshooting the most common hiccups.

What You Need Before You Start

  • A Samsung TV running Tizen (2016 or newer) or an LG TV running webOS (2015 or newer).
  • Stable internet connection – at least 10 Mbps download for HD streams, 25 Mbps for 4K.
  • A valid Beast IPTV subscription (login details are sent to your email after purchase).
  • A USB flash drive (optional) if you prefer sideloading the app.
  • A remote control or a compatible Bluetooth keyboard for easier navigation.

Installing Beast IPTV on Samsung Tizen

Samsung’s Tizen OS does not list third‑party IPTV apps in its official store, so you’ll need to sideload the Beast IPTV APK using the Smart Hub developer mode.

  1. Turn on your TV and navigate to Settings > General > External Device Manager > Device Connect Manager. Enable Developer Mode and set a password (e.g., 0000).
  2. On a computer, download the latest BeastIPTV_Tizen.spk package from beast-iptv.store/downloads.
  3. Insert the USB flash drive into the computer, copy the .spk file onto the root of the drive, then eject safely.
  4. Plug the USB into the TV’s USB port. Go to Apps > My Apps, press the Menu button on the remote, and select Install from USB. Locate the .spk file and confirm installation.
  5. After installation, the Beast IPTV icon appears in Apps. Launch it and sign in with your Beast IPTV credentials.
"Sideloading may sound technical, but once you enable developer mode the process is as simple as installing any other app from a USB drive," says a Beast IPTV support engineer.

Installing Beast IPTV on LG webOS

LG’s webOS offers a more straightforward path because the Beast IPTV app is available through the LG Content Store for most recent models.

  1. Press the Home button on your remote, then select LG Content Store.
  2. Navigate to Apps > Search and type "Beast IPTV".
  3. Select the official Beast IPTV app (publisher: Beast IPTV) and click Install. The app will download and appear on your home screen.
  4. Open the app, enter your username and password, and press Login.
  5. If the app does not appear, enable Developer Mode (Settings > General > About This TV > Developer Mode) and repeat the search.

Configuring the Beast IPTV App

After logging in, a few configuration steps ensure optimal playback and a personalized channel lineup.

  1. Go to Settings > Playback and select your preferred video quality (Auto, 1080p, 4K). Auto is recommended for variable bandwidth.
  2. Enable EPG (Electronic Program Guide) to view schedule information for each channel.
  3. Under Favorites, add the channels you watch most often for quick access.
  4. If you experience buffering, enable Hardware Decoding (if available) to offload processing to the TV’s GPU.
  5. Save your settings and restart the app to apply changes.

Troubleshooting Common Issues

1. App Fails to Launch

  • Verify that your TV firmware is up to date (Settings > Support > Software Update).
  • Clear the app cache: Settings > Apps > Beast IPTV > Clear Cache.
  • Re‑enter your login credentials; a typo in the password is the most frequent cause.

2. Black Screen or No Audio

  • Check that HDMI‑CEC is enabled (Settings > General > External Device Manager). Some TVs mute audio when CEC is disabled.
  • Switch the video output mode: Settings > Picture > HDMI UHD Color – turn it off for 1080p streams.
  • Restart your router to refresh the network handshake.

3. Buffering or Stuttering

  • Run a speed test on your TV (you can use the built‑in network diagnostics). If speed falls below 10 Mbps, consider a wired Ethernet connection.
  • Reduce the stream quality in the app’s Playback settings.
  • Close other apps running in the background, especially those that use Wi‑Fi (e.g., browsers, games).

4. Missing Channels or EPG Data

  • Refresh the channel list: Settings > Channels > Refresh List.
  • Make sure your Beast IPTV subscription is active; expired accounts lose access to premium channel packs.
  • Contact Beast IPTV support if the issue persists after a full app reinstall.
